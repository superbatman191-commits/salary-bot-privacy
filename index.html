salary-bot

salary-bot is a private, single-user Telegram bot. Its owner sends photos of his own weekly timesheets; the bot reads them, calculates the weekly pay, emails the result, records it in the owner's Google Sheet and archives the scan.

The bot is not offered to the public and serves only its owner.
